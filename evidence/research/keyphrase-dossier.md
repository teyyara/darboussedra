## Facts from the supplied brief
The house is the primary information architecture. The first milestone is an intentionally empty architectural world. Thirteen permanent spaces are required, including four individually named private rooms. A possible remembrance space for Yahya is a reserved future concept without an approved location. No accounts, backend, media storage, paid assets/services, analytics or public deployment are in scope. Scene, map and navigation must use shared room data; HTML controls and a WebGL fallback are required.

## Design implications
Use warm plaster, sand, natural wood, restrained terracotta and olive/sage; avoid clichés and excessive animation. Keep the rooms empty. Keep labels in HTML. Share registry data between map and navigable scene. Make keyboard/touch navigation first-class. Preserve room navigation without WebGL.
